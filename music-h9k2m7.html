This month · Prokofiev

SEP 2026 · Russian · program music · the on-ramp to the whole tradition

Play one piece per weekday morning at breakfast. Cycle Mon–Fri all month — by week 4 they know Prokofiev on sight. Say his name each morning.

Why him first: Peter and the Wolf was literally written to teach kids the orchestra. Every instrument is a character. It’s the on-ramp.

  1. MON
    Peter and the Wolf27 min · narratedSPOTIFY
    Each character = one instrument: Peter = strings, bird = flute, duck = oboe, cat = clarinet, grandpa = bassoon, wolf = horns, hunters = timpani. Ask them: which instrument for which animal?
  2. TUE
    TroikaLt. Kijé · 3 minSPOTIFY
    Sleigh bells and a big melody. They’ve heard it in commercials without knowing.
  3. WED
    Dance of the KnightsRomeo & Juliet · 5 minSPOTIFY
    The heavy stomping theme every action movie trailer steals. Kai will recognize it instantly.
  4. THU
    Classical Symphony · mvt 1Symphony No. 1 · 5 minSPOTIFY
    Prokofiev writing in Haydn’s style but sneaky-modern. Playful.
  5. FRI
    Cinderella · Grand Waltzballet · 4 minSPOTIFY
    Sweeping romantic ballet closer — Zada’s favorite kind of thing.
Connections → Prokofiev’s Russian modernism runs straight into film scoring — John Williams (Star Wars, Indiana Jones) borrows Prokofiev’s brass-heavy motif style constantly. When we get to Star Wars themes in the car this month, that’s the loop closing.

Watch or listen deeper

SEP DOCS + VIDEOS · ONE PER WEEKEND
  • Suzie Templeton — Peter and the Wolf (2006)WATCH · 33 MIN
    Oscar-winning stop-motion. Zero dialogue. The film-nerd version of the piece they’ll hear all month. Family-night alt.
  • Bernstein — Young People’s Concerts (CBS, 1960s)WATCH · YOUTUBE
    Bernstein explaining orchestra concepts to a hall of kids. The definitive kids-classical video series. Pick the episode “What Does Music Mean?” or “What is Orchestration?” to pair with Peter and the Wolf.
  • Classics for Kids · Prokofiev episodesPODCAST · 6 MIN
    Cincinnati Public Radio kids-classical show. Short weekday-morning length. Search “classics for kids prokofiev.”
  • Disney’s Peter and the Wolf (1946)WATCH · 14 MIN
    The animated short. Zada especially will love this version — the wolf is genuinely scary in the Disney way.

Zada & Kai teach

HOW THEY ACTUALLY LEARN THIS STUFF

Zada learns when she gets to be the teacher. Both of them lean in when their classmates are into something. Design around that.

Zada teaches

At end of the month, Zada picks one Prokofiev piece and gets to teach it to Debbie or grandma over FaceTime. What’s the story, which animal is which instrument. 3 minutes. She keeps the “expert” card until Nov.

Kai spots the borrow

Kai’s job is different: catch the composer in the wild. Prokofiev in a movie trailer, a video game, a commercial. When he flags one, that piece goes into the car playlist the next week.

How this works

Year 2 · walking the tree

SEP 2027 → AUG 2028 · POPULAR MUSIC ROOTS → NOW

After a year of composers, we walk the pop-music tree in order — roots first, then each branch. Each month connects backward (“where did this come from”) and forward (“what did this seed”). By August 2028, the kids can put BTS on the tree themselves.

  1. SEP
    Blues — the taproot
    ROOT Robert Johnson, Muddy Waters, BB King, Howlin’ Wolf. The 12-bar form. Everything downstream comes from here.
  2. OCT
    Jazz — the other taproot
    ROOT Armstrong → Ellington → Miles → Coltrane. Ties back to last year’s Ellington month.
  3. NOV
    Country
    ROOT Hank Williams, Johnny Cash, Dolly Parton, Willie Nelson. The third root.
  4. DEC
    Birth of rock ‘n’ roll
    50s Chuck Berry, Little Richard, Fats Domino, Ray Charles, Elvis. R&B + Rockabilly + Doo Wop.
  5. JAN
    The Beatles / Brit Invasion
    60s Beatles arc + Stones + Kinks. Where the whole modern songbook starts.
  6. FEB
    Motown & Soul
    60sBHM Aretha, Stevie, Marvin Gaye, Otis Redding, Sam Cooke. The other 60s.
  7. MAR
    Folk Rock / Singer-songwriter
    60s-70s Dylan, Simon & Garfunkel, CSN, Joni Mitchell, James Taylor.
  8. APR
    Funk / Disco
    70s James Brown, P-Funk, Sly, Bee Gees, Donna Summer, Chic. The dance-floor branch.
  9. MAY
    Punk / New Wave
    70s-80s Ramones, Clash, Talking Heads, Blondie. The reset button.
  10. JUN
    Hip-hop foundations
    80s-90s Sugar Hill Gang, Public Enemy, Tribe, Beasties, early hip-hop up to today.
  11. JUL
    Grunge & 90s alt
    90s Nirvana, Pearl Jam, Soundgarden, Radiohead, R.E.M.
  12. AUG
    Modern connections — where BTS actually lives
    NOW K-pop production traced backward: Motown vocal blend, hip-hop beats, disco arrangements, boy-band tradition. Kids place their own favorites on the tree. The payoff.
