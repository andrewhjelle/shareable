The Cloud Naan Test

CALIBRATION · UPDATED 2026-08-28

Every candidate answers one question: does this actually taste better than a Cloud Naan-tier enclave meal? If not, skip the premium. Steakhouses, tasting-menu theater, modern-ethnic-in-Randolph, and destination-ramen all fail this.

Primary sort = neighborhood, not cuisine. Restaurants are add-ons to already-planned trips. In Chinatown for a Sox game? Shoo Loong Kan. Uptown for a concert? Hai Yen or Demera after.

What we hunt: diaspora enclave institutions · chains from the motherland · craft-obsessive singletons where no diaspora exists · family-default rotations · side-menu stars.

What we skip: modern-fusion, tasting-menu theater, Americanized chains, tourist-preserved Bourdain-era spots, wait-required hype.

Honest gaps

CUISINES WITHOUT A COMMUNITY-VERIFIED CHICAGO ANCHOR
  • Turkish: Cafe Orchid appears closed as of Jul 2026. Chicago’s Turkish scene is thin; no true destination replacement right now. Turkitch and Ali Baba Doner are döner-only fallbacks. Fill only after a visit-verify.
  • Czech / Slovak: Klas closed 2016, demolished 2022. No true successor in Chicagoland. Bohemian House (River North) is modern-upscale, not enclave — skip. Don’t fake this one.
  • Indian modern-tasting: intentionally not covered. Rooh / Baar Baar / Basanti / Vajra all fail the Cloud Naan Test for Andrew. Devon is the actual answer.
  • Ramen destination: Akahoshi tried, “liked-not-amazing.” The lane is side-menu-star (Blowfish) + convenience anchors (Poké Burrito), not destination-ramen restaurants.
  • Steakhouses at spend tier: Chicago Cut / Bavette’s / Maple & Ash / RPM / Gibsons all legit-in-category but off Andrew’s lane. Home cooking + good butcher (Publican Quality Meats, Gepperth’s) is the alternative.

Anti-patterns

AUTO-FAIL · DON’T RECOMMEND
  • Modern-ethnic fusion in Randolph/West Loop: Rooh, Baar Baar, Basanti, Kimski, Fat Rice, New Rebozo. Cloud Naan Test fails.
  • Tasting-menu theater: Alinea, Next, Ever, Smyth, Oriole, Elske, Kumiko. Food theater is not the same as ambiance.
  • Americanized chains: Happy Lamb (worse than Shoo Loong Kan), P.F. Chang, Big Bowl, Uncle Julio’s.
  • Tourist-preserved Bourdain-era spots: Tank Noodle canonical. Community has moved on; Yelp lags.
  • Wait-required hype spots: Au Cheval (Small Cheval fixes it), Kasama tasting menu on weekends.
  • Illumination-style linear light walks: Morton Illumination, ZooLights, Lightscape. Cold + outdoor + passive triple-no.

How I vet

SOURCING HIERARCHY · NEVER YELP AGGREGATE
  1. LTHForum ethnic threads — 20+ years of serious Chicago-eater discussion. Primary source.
  2. Steve Dolinsky — Hungry Hound / The Feed. Field work in enclaves, not press junkets.
  3. Louisa Chu (Tribune) + Mike Sula (Reader) — deep-source ethnic reporting.
  4. Block Club Chicago — neighborhood reporting; caught Kashgar Uyghur in July 2026.
  5. Titus Ruscitti (Smokin Chokin blog) — obsessive documentarian of classic/ethnic Chicago.
  6. Google Maps reviews filtered to the community’s native language — the community eats where the community reviews.
  7. Reddit r/chicagofood — diaspora-source threads only, not “best of” posts.

Not sources: Yelp aggregate, Time Out, Eater, The Infatuation, Chicago Magazine best-of, TripAdvisor. All are trendy-detectors, not verified-good detectors. Yelp popularity for ethnic enclave = negative signal (Tank Noodle canonical).
