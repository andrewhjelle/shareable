What we learned

  • Meat matters. Using pre-formed burger patties (leaner than bulk ground chuck): don’t drain the fat. Add the diced tomatoes (or an extra splash of broth) or the mix goes dry.
  • Salt goes in early. 1 tsp with the spice bloom, more at the end if needed. Salting only at the end is easy to miss entirely.
  • Cinnamon needs a real pinch. Bumped to ¼ tsp — 1/8 tsp didn’t come through.
  • Chili powder: ancho preferred; Kashmiri, chipotle, or generic all work. Use what you have.
  • Oregano: regular oregano is fine. Mexican is more citrusy but not required.
  • Cheese: shredded whatever. Queso fresco / cotija traditional, mozzarella works.
  • Potato + carrot skipped for time — still great as pure taco meat.
  • Cook time is short. Beef browns in 4–6 min. Total ~15 min for the quick version.

              Full recipe

              Quick picadillo

              1. Dice 1 white onion. Have your minced garlic + spices measured out and lined up.
              2. Brown 1–1.25 lb ground beef (or 4 patties broken up) in a wide skillet over medium-high, 4–6 min. Break it up fine as it cooks.
              3. If bulk 80/20: drain excess fat, leave ~1 tbsp. If pre-formed patties (leaner): don’t drain.
              4. Add the diced onion. Cook 4–5 min until translucent.
              5. Add 2 tsp minced garlic, stir 30 sec.
              6. Add spices: 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano, ¼ tsp cinnamon, 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp black pepper, 1 tsp kosher salt. Stir 30 sec.
              7. Add 2 tbsp tomato paste. Stir into the meat, cook 1 min until it darkens.
              8. Add ¼ cup water or beef broth (or a 14 oz can of diced tomatoes if using lean meat or you want it stew-style).
              9. Cover, simmer 5–8 min on low.
              10. Fish out the bay leaf. Taste. Add up to ½ tsp more salt if flat.
              11. Serve on warm tortillas with the topping bar.

              Full stew variant

              At step 8, add all of these instead of just water: 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes, 2 yukon golds small-diced, 1 carrot small-diced, small handful raisins (optional Cuban touch). Simmer covered 15–20 min until potato is tender.
