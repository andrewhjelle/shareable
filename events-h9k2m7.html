Cheap-tickets playbook

HOW TO ACTUALLY GET IN CHEAP
  • Gametime app: day-of pricing for sports + some concerts. Real drops 2–6 hrs before start. Set price alerts, not searches.
  • TickPick: like StubHub, ~15% lower fees. Cross-check both for anything.
  • SeatGeek deal alerts: set a per-ticket max on any specific event; they ping when the market crosses your threshold.
  • Team/venue resale first: Chicago Fire, CSO, Lyric all have their own resale platforms that undercut third-party. Always check the team/venue site before StubHub.
  • Ravinia lawn tickets: $30–50 adult, kids often free, bring a blanket + picnic. The cheapest way to see a name act in Chicago.
  • Grant Park Music Festival (summer only, next season): free at Pritzker Pavilion, full orchestra, mid-June through mid-August. Set a reminder for May 2027.
  • CSO Family Matinees at Symphony Center: ~$25/seat, 55 min, 5–12 age band. Andrew’s concert-anchor slot on the music page.
  • Miss-Messi hedge (or its equivalent): for star-driven events, injury news 24–48 hr out cratrers resale prices. Only for the risk-tolerant.
  • Standing room: at Metro, Thalia Hall, Lincoln Hall — cheapest way in, best for the kids-and-Debbie later Zada is old enough for a non-seated show.

Price curve

WHEN THE FLOOR HITS · RESALE-MARKET SHAPE
  • T−2 wk → 72 hr: aspirational listings. Sellers post high, few sell. Don’t buy unless real sellout risk.
  • T−72 → 24 hr: first honest drop. Sellers who won’t attend get nervous. Typically 15–30% off peak.
  • T−12 → 4 hr: the sweet spot. Sellers realize $0 is the alternative. Another 20–40% drop. Gametime’s “last-minute mode” is exactly this window.
  • T−3 hr → gate: absolute floor, but cheap seats disappear FIRST because scavengers grab them. Wait too long = shut out.
  • After doors: coin flip. Sometimes a second dip on remaining inventory, sometimes prices tick up as buyers panic.
  • BTS-tier demand: curve is flatter. Floor stays higher than a Cubs game. Sweet spot ~4–6 PM for a 6:30 doors.
  • Star-driven sports: injury news 24–48 hr out cratrers resale. If Messi is questionable Friday of game week, buy on FUD.
  • Fee traps: Gametime shows all-in prices; StubHub/Vivid add 25–30% at checkout. TickPick has no buyer fees. Always compare net, not sticker.
  • Where the curve doesn’t exist: Ravinia lawn, Old Town School, CSO, small clubs. Buy at face when you decide — no secondary game to play.

Anti-patterns

DON’T
  • Don’t buy face-value from a scalper site until you’ve checked the team/venue resale. 40% price haircut is normal.
  • Don’t overpay for parking. Downtown = transit. Ravinia = shuttle from Metra.
  • Don’t stack two big outings in one weekend. Kai and Zada both burn out. One anchor event per weekend.
  • Don’t force attendance at anything a kid’s cold on. The music page principle applies here too — watching a livestream now, in-person next year, is a valid pathway.
