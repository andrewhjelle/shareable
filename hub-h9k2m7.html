    • Clean the 3D printer30–45 MIN
      Generic FDM cleaning path. Tell me the model (Bambu / Prusa / Ender / Anycubic…) and I’ll swap in specifics; the below works on most.
      1. Power off + let cool before anything mechanical. Nozzle steps come later hot.
      2. Build plate: remove, wipe with 91%+ isopropyl alcohol (both sides). PEI plate = plastic scraper only for stuck bits, no metal. Glass = razor at a shallow angle works. Wash with dish soap + warm water every few months; oils from fingers kill adhesion.
      3. Nozzle exterior: heat to 200-220°C, brass wire brush (never steel) to knock off cooked filament. Wipe with a folded shop towel while warm.
      4. Clogged nozzle: cold pull. Heat to 220°C, insert cleaning filament (or nylon), extrude a few mm, drop temp to 90°C, wait, yank the filament out fast. Repeat until pull comes out clean. If still clogged, replace the nozzle ($5, 5 min).
      5. Extruder gears: disengage the idler, use a soft brush or compressed air to clear plastic dust/shavings from between the teeth. Filament dust is the #1 cause of skipped extrusion.
      6. Rails / linear rods: wipe clean with a dry lint-free cloth, then light lube. Use white lithium grease OR PTFE-based lube. Never WD-40 (solvent, evaporates, attracts more dust).
      7. Belts: compressed air / soft brush along the belt path. Check tension — if a belt sags or twangs low, follow the printer’s tensioner procedure.
      8. Fans: compressed air short bursts (hold blades still with a finger so they don’t spin up and damage the bearing). Both hotend fan and part-cooling fan.
      9. Bowden tube (if Bowden printer): pull tube, check the end at the hotend for melt/wear/discoloration. Trim 1–2 mm off if damaged, reseat.
      10. Firmware + level check: after any bed removal or nozzle work, re-level (auto or manual per your printer). Do a first-layer test print to confirm.

      Consumables to keep on hand: spare nozzles (0.4 mm brass, ~$1 each in packs of 10) · PTFE tube (Bowden) · brass wire brush · 91%+ IPA · PLA cleaning filament.
