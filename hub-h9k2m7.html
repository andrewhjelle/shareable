    Order: hub first, then floodlight, then keypad. Everything downstream needs the hub online.

    Stage 1 — Hub online

    1. Ethernet cable from hub → LAN port on router. Then power. 60 sec to solid (not blinking) status light.
    2. Wyze app: Home → + → Add Device → Home Security.
    3. Not found? Unplug 10 sec, plug back, hit “Search again” within 30 sec of boot.

    Stage 2 — Keypad

    1. Hub green? Then Home Monitoring → Settings → Devices → Add → Keypad.
    2. Pop battery cover, hold reset pin 5 sec until LED flashes.
    3. Pair within ~10 ft of hub; mount farther after.
    4. If pairing fails twice: fresh AAs before anything else.

    Stage 3 — Garage floodlight

    1. Confirm model: original Cam Floodlight (v2 add-on to Cam v3) vs. Floodlight Pro (standalone). Setup differs.
    2. Switch stays ON (constant power). Tape it or rewire always-on.

    4. Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz credentials only. Won’t join 5 GHz even w/ same SSID name. On mesh, disable band steering during setup.

    Fallback isolation: phone on mobile hotspot (2.4 GHz), pair device there first to confirm it’s not broken, then move to home Wi-Fi.
