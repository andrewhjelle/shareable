06House — still to do

Absorbed from the house page

Full deep guides (step-by-steps, materials, gates) live at roof-leak, vent-flashing, shower-cement. Top actions here.
  • Deck wasps: break the re-nesting cycle
    ~$25
    1. Early morning (cool, they’re sluggish): knock nest into bag, seal.
    2. Scrub attachment spot with soapy water — kills the pheromone marker. Most-skipped step, the reason they come back.
    3. Outlet: foam gasket behind cover + in-use bubble cover so the cavity is gone.
    4. Light: re-seat glass housing, plug gaps. Dust insecticide only if near either fixture — never liquid.
    5. Sept/Oct: seal remaining deck crevices while nests are empty.
  • Office AC: clean the Shinco, then re-route the ducting
    1–2 hrs · $25–70
    This order — no point routing dirty airflow.
    1. Clean first: filter (rinse, dry fully), coils (soft brush + vac), condensate plug drained.
    2. Re-route exhaust: shortest, straightest run to the exit. Every extra foot radiates heat back.
    3. Wrap what remains exposed: foil wrap on bare inches, foil tape (not duct tape) on joints.
  • Shower curb: glue the three cement-board pieces
    2 hrs
    Gate check: PL Premium on hand (NOT X-15), RedGard uniformly red, liner coverage decides screws-or-no.
  • Roof odds-and-ends trip: cable-mast bead + AC line-set
    30 min · ~$20
    1. First, when HD order arrives: squeeze the foam insulation. Black rubbery = go; gray polyethylene = swap for rubber line-set sleeves (~$10) before this trip.
    2. Do BEFORE the recoat so 687 goes over fresh seals.
    3. (a) Solar Seal bead at cable entry + mast-cone cap seam.
    4. (b) Copy neighbor’s 8/8 fix in white: new foam sleeves on AC suction line, UV tape wrap, Solar Seal where lines enter. NO black mastic (ghosts through white).
    5. Top-of-cone: gap wider than 1/2"? Foam backer rod first. Work bead around each line, then middle. Dome the top so water sheds.
  • Recoat 4th-floor roof — this season
    Aug 30–31 · half day × 2
    All materials ordered 8/9. Gates: patches cured (Aug 17+ ✔), dry 3-day stretch, 50°F+, mornings only (2+ hrs before nightfall — dew kills fresh coating). 144 sq ft ≈ 90 min rolling per coat.
    1. Sweep + shop-vac whole roof, edges + penetrations especially (30 min).
    2. Prep wide cracks: wire-brush, alcohol wipe, Solar Seal + ElastoTape + skim (45–60 min).
    3. Reinforce remaining penetrations: AC pad edges, drain, parapet base.
    4. Coat 1 (morning): stir with paddle, chip-brush penetrations first, then roll flats ONE direction. ~1.4 gal. DO NOT THIN.
    5. Cure overnight.
    6. Coat 2 next morning, perpendicular. This coat is the waterproofing.
    7. Stay off a week, watch known spots through 2–3 rains.
  • 3rd-floor deck: shave gutter-edge dam, rebuild seam, recoat band
    Pairs w/ wasp trip
    Root cause: raised sealant bead before the gutter dams water. Shave (don’t rip) → 289 + 296 ElastoTape three-course → water test → recoat 90 sq ft band. Boards off a week for cure.
  • Deck slider: fish out toy, clean track, clear weep holes
    30 min
    Weep-hole unplug worked in the 8/9 storm. Check other 1–2 weeps on the outside face. Silicone spray on rollers, NOT WD-40 (oil + grit = grinding paste).
  • Open 4th-floor ceiling for the mold verdict
    30–45 min
    Score-and-snap, low dust, N95. Crew patches to joist centers anyway — bigger exploratory hole costs nothing.
    1. Kill ceiling circuit at breaker if fixture/wiring near stain.
    2. Find joists: rare-earth magnet in a line — sticks at every screw.
    3. Draw rectangle: joist centerline to centerline; ~2" past stain into clean board.
    4. Mist lines lightly — damp gypsum cuts near dust-free.
    5. Score deep 3–4 firm passes, shop-vac trailing blade. Blade <= 3/4" out (wires up there).
    6. Palm-punch mid-piece, pull down gently, slice back paper.
    7. Bag piece immediately — but LOOK at back face first. Evidence.
    8. Photograph everything.
    9. Meter framing several spots. Under 15% = dry. Over 19% = water still arriving.
  • Moisture-meter the framing in all three areas
