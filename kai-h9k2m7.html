Home trackDoesn’t wait on the school · start this week
  • Khan Academy, 7th grade math. 20 minutes a day, weekdays. Andrew or Debbie sits with him for the first two weeks; after that he runs it and reports the unit he finished at dinner. Structure is the point: it’s a fixed slot, not an optional extra.
  • Order of units. Negative numbers and signed-number arithmetic first (the real gap between 6th and 7th), then proportional relationships and percents, then multi-step equations and inequalities. Geometry, probability, and statistics last.
  • If he wants rigor rather than catch-up: AoPS Prealgebra. Builds from why, not procedure, which is the Montessori-compatible choice.
  • What it produces. In four to six weeks, unit-mastery evidence on paper and a kid who walks into a trial placement warmed up. If the school says no, he progresses anyway and the fall MAP has a tailwind.
What 7th grade adds over 6th: proportional relationships as the organizing idea (percent change, tax, interest), full signed-number arithmetic, multi-step equations and inequalities, angle relationships and circles, probability and sampling. In a Montessori school 7th is also the first year of the adolescent program, which shifts from materials to abstraction and usually runs a published curriculum. Which one Intercultural uses is still an open question on the thread.
