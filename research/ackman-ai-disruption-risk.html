From activist raids to durable long-term holdings

  • Ackman describes his investing shifting over time toward prioritizing durable, hard-to-disrupt business quality rather than short-term liquid positions.
  • He traces this back to Pershing Square's early days, when an unreturned phone call to Wendy's CEO led to the firm's first major activist bet: buying Wendy's stock to force a spinoff of its more valuable Tim Hortons subsidiary.
