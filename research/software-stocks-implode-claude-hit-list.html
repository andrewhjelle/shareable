If you only read one thing

  • IBM fell 13% (worst day since 2000, $31B in market cap) after Anthropic said Claude can modernize COBOL code that runs 95% of US ATMs, the third Anthropic announcement in February to tank a different stock sector.
  • A viral 'Catrini' Substack piece describing an AI-driven economic death spiral briefly tanked financial stocks Monday, until it emerged the amended co-author is a hedge-fund manager who confirmed short positions in the named companies; Citadel Securities data instead shows software-engineer job postings up roughly 10% year-over-year.
  • SCOTUS struck down Trump's IEEPA tariffs 6-3, Trump immediately invoked a 15% global tariff under Section 122 of the 1974 trade act, and roughly 100 US data-center projects are currently facing local opposition.

02Markets shift from 'when' to 'if'

Markets shift from 'when' to 'if'

  • Chamath argues public markets have moved from debating when a company's cash flows will be disrupted to debating whether they will exist at all, which pushes investors toward lower price-to-earnings and revenue multiples and higher discount rates across the board.

“a when moment. The market is very much in an if mode.”

03The viral AI-doom Substack, and the pushback

The viral AI-doom Substack, and the pushback

  • A Substack piece describing a fictional 2028 AI-driven economic collapse went viral and coincided with a Monday selloff in financial stocks.

04Is AI actually eliminating software jobs?

Is AI actually eliminating software jobs?

  • Critics point to Anthropic's own $570,000 open software-engineer listing as a contradiction if the company expects to obsolete the role.
  • Citadel Securities data cited on the show instead shows software-engineer job postings rising roughly 10% year-over-year, which the hosts read through Aaron Levy's framing of Jevons paradox: cheaper software engineering increases demand for it rather than eliminating the role.
