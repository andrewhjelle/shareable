02Drone warfare and AI targeting

Drone warfare and AI targeting

  • Michael says roughly 70% of casualties in the Russia-Ukraine war come from drones, calling drone-on-drone and robot-on-robot warfare the near-term future of conflict.
  • He connects this to AI's expanding role in missile defense, citing a Golden Dome scenario where a Chinese hypersonic missile gives roughly 90 seconds of warning -- a window he says exceeds human reaction time and visual discrimination.

03Pentagon brands Anthropic a supply-chain risk

Pentagon brands Anthropic a supply-chain risk

  • Bloomberg reported the Pentagon formally notified Anthropic it had been deemed a supply chain risk, a designation Jason Calacanis says has previously applied only to Russian and Chinese companies like Huawei, following the Department of War's cancellation of a $200 million Anthropic contract.
  • Michael says the dispute centered on two clauses: Anthropic's restrictions on fully autonomous weapons and its concerns about mass surveillance of Americans.
  • Michael says reviewing the existing contracts as the department's new AI portfolio lead, he found terms barring use of the model to plan a kinetic strike or move a satellite.

“This has never happened to an American company. It has happened to Russian companies. uh and Chinese companies Huawei and for background the department of war cancelled Anthropic $200 million contract on Friday”

How the contract actually worked

  • Michael explains the technical setup: Anthropic's model sits in AWS GovCloud and is served to the department through Palantir, but Anthropic retains the control plane for the model and refreshes it, meaning it can alter the model weights at will.

The fallout: single-vendor risk and Anthropic's growth
