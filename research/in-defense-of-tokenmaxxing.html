The debate splits into a believer camp and a critic camp

03Whittemore's counter: agent management has no existing experts

Whittemore's counter: agent management has no existing experts

  • His central argument is that managing agents is a new knowledge-work primitive, distinct from prompting a chatbot, and that no best practices exist for it yet.
  • In his framing, the only people ahead of the curve are the ones who have experimented more, which makes token-heavy experimentation a legitimate form of R&D rather than waste.
