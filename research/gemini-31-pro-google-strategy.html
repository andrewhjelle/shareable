If you only read one thing

  • Gemini 3.1 Pro leads 13 of 16 benchmarks and posted the largest single-generation reasoning jump on ARC-AGI-2 on record (31.1% to 77.1% in 90 days), while pricing at roughly a seventh of Opus 4.6.
  • On real-world, tool-using benchmarks like GDPval, Opus 4.6 still leads by 289 Elo points — the video frames it as Google building the better 'engine' while Anthropic built the better 'car.'
  • Gemini's Deep Think mode used pure reasoning to disprove a decade-old math conjecture and helped Isomorphic Labs more than double AlphaFold 3's protein-prediction accuracy, which the video reads as Google optimizing for scientific breakthroughs rather than daily-workflow share.

The benchmark story

  • Gemini 3.1 Pro leads on 13 of 16 benchmarks tracked in the video.
  • The standout is ARC-AGI-2, a test of novel reasoning rather than retrieval, where the model's score roughly doubled from its November predecessor in 90 days, the largest single-generation reasoning gain the video attributes to any frontier model family.

Cost, and where Opus still wins

  • Gemini 3.1 Pro is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $12 per million output tokens, roughly 7.5x cheaper than Opus 4.6 on input.
  • But on GDPval, a benchmark of expert-level office and financial tasks with tools, Opus leads by 289 Elo points.

04Not all 'hard' work is reasoning-hard

Not all 'hard' work is reasoning-hard

  • The video argues most business problems aren't bottlenecked on reasoning at all, it names 'effort problems' like auditing thousands of vendor contracts or migrating a legacy COBOL codebase, where the per-step thinking is simple but the volume is what's hard, as the kind of work agentic tools like Opus are built for instead.

“Auditing 3,000 vendor contracts for compliance changes. Migrating a legacy code base with 2 million lines of COBOL.”

Practical takeaway: route by problem type

  • The video's closing advice is to stop asking which model is smartest overall and instead map which model handles which specific task type most reliably in your own domain.

Commentary (ours)

The claim that most knowledge work is dominated by effort, coordination, and judgment problems rather than pure reasoning is presented as the video's own framework, built from the presenter's read of his own workday, it's a useful lens, not an industry-measured statistic (ours).
