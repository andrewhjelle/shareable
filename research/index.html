Trend signals (ours, hedged)

  • (ours) With model releases now landing roughly weekly and per-token prices falling, the harness, distribution, and knowledge layer look more likely than raw benchmark leadership to decide which lab keeps enterprise share into 2027.
  • (ours) The volume of agent-safety writing has shifted from model alignment toward system-property design (kill switches, judge models, agent identity, procurement gates), suggesting agent security is professionalizing into an infrastructure discipline that enterprise buyers will start pricing into contracts.
  • (ours) Token-metered enterprise pricing, agent-access controls, and reserved-capacity language in vendor contracts are moving faster than most large-company procurement processes, so a wave of renegotiation and buyer-side leverage loss looks likely inside the next few quarters.
  • (ours) The Karpathy hire, Amodei's compounding-advantage comment, and the reported Brin strike team together imply the next 6-12 months will produce visible tests of whether AI-on-AI acceleration is a real curve, which if it lands would invalidate the augmentation narrative rebuilt this quarter.
  • (ours) Data-center opposition, encyclical-driven distribution concerns, and Sanders and Cuban token-tax proposals suggest 2026 AI policy pressure will move from safety-of-models toward distribution-of-benefits, an axis the current no-regulation consensus underestimates.
