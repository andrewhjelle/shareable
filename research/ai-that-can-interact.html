OpenAI's consulting arm goes live

  • OpenAI's planned consulting arm, the OpenAI Deployment Company (DeployCo), is now official: a joint venture with 19 partners across consulting, private equity, and finance, with TPG as lead investor.
  • The business is a forward-deployed engineer shop pairing OpenAI developers with major clients, and its initial headcount comes from acquiring engineering firm Tomorrow, which brings about 150 staff already experienced in deploying AI solutions.

Anthropic moves to void gray-market stock claims

  • Anthropic updated its support documentation to state it does not permit SPVs to hold its stock and that any transfer to an SPV is void, warning that third parties selling access to 'Anthropic shares' are either committing fraud or offering something that may have no value.

Washington backs off drug-style AI approval, leaves Jensen off the China trip

04Thinking Machines pitches a new way to talk to AI

Thinking Machines pitches a new way to talk to AI

  • Thinking Machines Lab, founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, released 'interaction models,' described as trained from scratch for real-time, continuous exchange rather than glued onto a turn-based core.
  • The lab's blog post argues today's turn-based systems create a 'collaboration bottleneck' where the model can't perceive what a user is doing while it generates a response.
  • Architecturally, the interaction model processes streams in 200-millisecond micro-turns and pairs with a separate background model that handles longer reasoning, browsing, and tool use.

“argues that today's AI systems create what they call a collaboration bottleneck”

said at 13:10

“Instead of that turn-based system, where inputs and outputs are, in their words, flattened into one-order token sequence, their model processes streams in 200-ms micro-turns”

said at 14:11
05Why they had to invent new benchmarks

Why they had to invent new benchmarks

  • Because current commercial real-time voice APIs cannot proactively speak based on what they see -- the example given is a model asked to count push-ups that just waits silently for an audio cue that never comes -- Thinking Machines had to create two new internal benchmarks, TimeSpeak and QSpeak, to measure whether a model can initiate speech at the right moment with the right content.

“they ended up having to create two internal benchmarks to measure new proactive audio capabilities”

said at 18:18

Commentary (ours)

(ours) The host frames the interaction-model announcement as likely to be copied by other labs quickly, citing OpenAI's same-day demo of a background agent updating a Kanban board during a meeting.

Whether 'interaction models' specifically, rather than background agents generally, becomes the actual differentiator is still unproven outside TML's own demo videos, which the host notes are unpolished researcher clips rather than production launches.
