How to win when software is not a moat | Evan Spiegel (Snapchat CEO)

SpeakerEvan Spiegel · Snap Watchyoutu.be/-7Yol5vX5xw · 70 min SourceRecording of this talk at AI Engineer (2026), published on YouTube by the conference. Quotes are verbatim from the talk transcript with the time they were said.

If you only read one thing

  • Spiegel says Snap learned 15 years ago that 'software is not a moat' since features are easily cloned, so it invested in platforms/ecosystems and vertically integrated hardware (Specs) as harder-to-copy defenses

01Software is not a moat, so Snap builds platforms and hardware instead

Software is not a moat, so Snap builds platforms and hardware instead

  • Spiegel says Snap learned 15 years ago that software features are easily cloned and therefore not defensible on their own, a lesson he says the industry is relearning with AI.
  • In response, Snap invested in ecosystems (creators, AR lens developers) and vertically integrated hardware.
  • He also credits Snapchat's early growth to connecting users to their closest friend or partner rather than maximizing total network size, contrasted with TikTok's strategy of spending billions of dollars to subsidize both sides of its video marketplace.

02The design team's operating model

The design team's operating model

  • Spiegel describes Snap's design team as oscillating between nine and 12 people, deliberately kept flat even as the wider company serves almost a billion users.
  • In the early days he avoided hiring dedicated product managers, instead expecting designers to do that work themselves.
  • He also says design functions as a mandatory bottleneck: nothing ships without design approval, which he credits with keeping the product experience cohesive.

03Snapchat Plus and product invention stories

Snapchat Plus and product invention stories

  • Spiegel says Snapchat+ has reached 25 million subscribers and a revenue run rate of more than $1 billion.
  • He also recounts discovering that pressing and holding to open a Snap while taking a screenshot triggered a detectable 'finger lost contact with the screen' event on iPhone, which let early Snapchat notify a sender that their photo had been screenshotted even without an official Apple API for it.

04AI, customers, and Spiegel's contrarian take

AI, customers, and Spiegel's contrarian take

  • Asked about a contrarian view that consumer builders shouldn't talk to customers, Spiegel says builders should talk to customers and share ideas as quickly and frequently as possible, even without adopting their literal feature requests.
  • His stated contrarian take is that humanity matters more than the technology itself, since human comfort and adoption, not raw capability, will determine how AI actually gets deployed.
