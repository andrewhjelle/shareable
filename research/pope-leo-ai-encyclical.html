The document and its context

  • Pope Leo XIV's first encyclical, released the day before this episode, triggered what Whittemore calls the most social discourse about AI as a global societal force he's seen in a 24-hour span.
  • In his first address after being elected in May 2025, Leo framed the Church's response to AI in terms of an earlier industrial revolution, invoking the Church's social teaching to defend human dignity, justice, and labor.

How commentators split

Commentary (ours)

(ours) Whittemore is explicit that he's putting words in the Pope's mouth to distill a document longer than most books, and the split reactions he cites -- Timnit Gebru, Blake Scholl, Chris Murphy, Christopher Hale, Dean Ball -- read the same text as vindicating opposite priors.

That pattern (a nuanced document, quickly mined for one quotable line by people who hadn't necessarily read the rest) seems likely to repeat with future AI-policy statements from non-technical institutions, worth watching for on the next one.
