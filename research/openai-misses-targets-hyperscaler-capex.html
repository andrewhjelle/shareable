03Chamath: everything is power-constrained

Chamath: everything is power-constrained

  • Chamath argues that missed forecasts across the industry trace to power supply, not demand, and that a growing backlog in grid components (transformers, gas turbines) will make the bottleneck worse before it gets better.

“Everything in this market is power constrained. The reason that these folks may miss a number or a forecast have nothing to do with demand.”
