01The old theory of the firm breaks

The old theory of the firm breaks

  • Salim Ismail traces organizational design back to Ronald Coase's 1937 paper 'The Nature of the Firm,' which held that coordination costs make work cheaper inside a company than outside it.
  • He argues AI collapses execution costs to near zero, so that theory no longer applies.
  • Separately, he cites a figure that 80-plus percent of AI projects inside companies are currently failing, which he attributes to bolting AI onto legacy, human-centric approval workflows rather than rebuilding them AI-native.

02Organize around intelligence, not hierarchy

Organize around intelligence, not hierarchy

  • Ismail and Diamandis define the 'organizational singularity' as replacing hierarchy-based coordination with a structure architected around intelligence.
  • In their framework the company's massive transformative purpose (MTP) becomes an enforced protocol for AI and human agents rather than a wall poster, wrapped around a six-layer intelligence stack (purpose, sensing, interpretation, decision, orchestration, learning) with a governance loop of logging, rollback, and human review.

“It needs to be architected around intelligence, not around hierarchy.”

03The disruption warning

The disruption warning

  • Diamandis frames the risk as a direct challenge to every CEO and C-suite member: identify whether a high-margin line of the business could be replicated by a small outside team using current agentic tools, because if so, someone will attempt it.

04Projected headcount and performance impact

Projected headcount and performance impact

  • Ismail estimates an average company will eventually run on roughly 20-25% of its current workforce once this transition completes, with the exact share depending on how physical or regulatory-bound the business is.
  • He gives a specific client example: OpenExO estimated Fermi America could run a power plant with about 80 people instead of 800.
  • He also states that a properly running AI-native digital twin of a workflow should deliver 100x or higher performance improvement per year.

05Early evidence and resistance

Early evidence and resistance

  • As evidence this is already underway rather than speculative, Ismail cites Cognition Labs' annual recurring revenue growing 73 times after going fully AI-native.
  • He also describes internal resistance to the shift, citing a figure that 44% of Gen Z workers sabotage AI systems with bad information to protect their jobs.

Commentary (ours)

(ours) Ismail's headcount and revenue figures (20-25%, 73x, 800-to-80) are his own firm's client estimates and case examples, not independently audited numbers -- useful as directional signal, not as benchmarks to cite without verification.

The talk also functions as a pitch for OpenExO's paid consulting process and forthcoming book/Claude skill, which is worth weighing when judging how aggressively the numbers are framed.
