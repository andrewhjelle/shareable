If you only read one thing

  • Anthropic expects its first-ever profitable quarter for any AI lab, OpenAI generated about $1B more revenue than Anthropic in Q1, and Google's Gemini app hit 900M monthly active users with monthly token volume up 700% since last May (c1, c2, c3, c4).

Profitability and scale, accelerating together

02Model capability: an 80-year-old math problem and a return to research

Model capability: an 80-year-old math problem and a return to research

  • An internal OpenAI model, described as a general-purpose LLM with no specific math training, disproved a decades-old conjecture in a 1946 Paul Erdos geometry problem about points on a plane.
  • Fields medalist Tim Gowers, who co-authored the companion paper, called it the first clear example of AI solving a well-known unsolved problem rather than an obscure one.
  • In a related model-development story, Andrej Karpathy announced he is returning to work, this time at Anthropic, on what colleagues describe as recursive self-improvement -- using Claude to accelerate pre-training research itself.

04A federal AI order drafted, then killed in a day

A federal AI order drafted, then killed in a day

  • A draft Trump administration executive order would have required AI companies to submit models to the government 90 days ahead of public release for safety benchmarking, with industry reportedly pushing for 14 days instead.
  • Hours before a planned signing ceremony with tech executives invited, the order was scuttled; Politico reported that David Sacks personally intervened with Trump, arguing that companies were already cooperating and that mandatory federal review would slow US progress against China.
