If you only read one thing

  • Yang argues UBI must come before any 'universal high income,' citing Anthropic's public estimate that roughly 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs get automated in 1-5 years, and says his original $1,000/month proposal is now too low against ~$84,000 GDP per capita.

01UBI has to come before universal high income

UBI has to come before universal high income

  • Responding to Elon Musk's prediction of 'universal high income' alongside social unrest, Yang says UBI has to be the intermediate step, since he doesn't see a path to UHI without a major political realignment first.
  • He cites Anthropic's public estimate on entry-level automation as evidence the clock is short, and reworks his 2020 UBI number upward against current GDP-per-capita figures.
