Build & deploy AI-powered apps

SpeakerPaige Bailey · Google DeepMind Watchyoutu.be/G_bHFmEAarM
Product & UXGenerative mediaVoice & realtime

If you only read one thing

  • Gemini is multimodal for both inputs and outputs, and AI Studio tools like URL context and code execution let small models like Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite analyze images (e.g. bounding boxes) accurately and cheaply.
  • Gemini Live supports real-time screen-share and voice conversation with dynamic language/accent switching and function calling, at a lower price point than stitching together separate STT/LLM/TTS pipelines.
  • Genie 3 generates explorable worlds frame-by-frame with no physics engine, as a composition of models (Nano Banana, VEO, Gemini); it outputs pixels only, not 3D meshes or game assets.
01Gemini handles more input and output types than most models

The talk frames Gemini's differentiator as multimodality on both sides: it accepts video, images, audio, text, and code as input, and can output multiple modalities beyond just text and code, unlike most other models on the market which are limited to text/code output and static image input.

“it's multimodal both for inputs and also multimodal in terms of outputs”

02Grounding tools extend a model past its training cutoff

AI Studio exposes toggleable tools including grounding with Google Search, grounding with Google Maps, and URL context, which the speaker describes as letting you feed a list of URLs into the context window as a lightweight retrieval mechanism without standing up a vector database.

“URL context, which gives you kind of like poor man's retrieval”

▶ watch at 07:09
03Small models plus tools do vision tasks cheaply

In a live compare-mode demo, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite with code execution enabled correctly drew bounding boxes around Lego bricks in an image, completing the task and beating a larger Gemini 3 Flash model on speed, at a token cost the speaker described as well under a fraction of a cent.

“Gemini 3.1 flashlight was able to get it right out of the gate, which is pretty wild.”

▶ watch at 14:59
04Gemini Live enables real-time screen/voice agents

Gemini Live lets a user share their screen, a video feed, or audio with the model and hold a dynamic conversation, including custom function calls, automatic function responses, and Google Search grounding as a callable tool, demonstrated live with screen-sharing, language switching, and accent changes.

“Gemini Live gives you the ability to share your screen, share a video feed, share audio with the model, and have a conversation with it dynamically”

▶ watch at 19:11
05AI Studio Build generates full-stack apps; Genie 3 generates explorable worlds but not 3D assets; Gemma 4 is free to download

The Build feature in AI Studio creates and deploys apps with database and authentication support, compared by the speaker to v0.dev or Lovable. Genie 3 is described as DeepMind's model for generating new worlds from a text/image prompt, built as a composition of models (Nano Banana, VEO, Gemini) rather than one model, and confirmed in Q&A to output only pixels frame-by-frame with no physics engine, not 3D game meshes. Gemma 4 models can be downloaded, fine-tuned, and run for free under an Apache 2 license.

“we have another feature called build, which, if you've played with v0.dev or Lovable, feels very similar”
