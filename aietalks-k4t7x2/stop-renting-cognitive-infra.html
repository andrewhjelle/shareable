Stop Renting Your Cognitive Infrastructure

takeInfra & servingLocal models

Gives a concrete decision rule (rent pre-product-market-fit, own post-PMF) plus real failure modes worth checking before committing a product's inference spend to a single rented API: stolen keys, enterprise audit/compliance redlines on ...

If you only read one thing

  • Warns that rented inference costs balloon unpredictably: a major retailer spent nearly $200M with Anthropic before building its own infrastructure, and Uber's CTO burned a year's token budget in four months.
  • A stolen API key spiked his own inference bill toward $10,000 within three weeks before it was caught.
  • Recommends renting inference only pre-product-market-fit; once post-PMF or budgeted as an enterprise project, build owned infrastructure ('rent to learn, own to earn').

A stolen API key nearly cost him $100,000

  • He describes his own app, Ultra Sono, having its API key stolen three weeks before the talk, with an attacker in China draining the endpoint and costs climbing toward $10,000 before his co-founder caught and stopped it.

“my key got stolen. Someone in China got hold of it and then was sucking my endpoint dry.”

03Self-hosting worked for him, but hit walls for enterprise clients
Slide at 03:40 · click any slide to enlarge

Self-hosting worked for him, but hit walls for enterprise clients

  • He moved his own app to a self-owned DGX box and it worked well enough to run additional agents, but three enterprise prospects (a fund, a hospital, a tax practice) each hit a different blocker with rented/third-party inference: loss of rate-limit control, a third-party vendor dependency getting redlined in an audit, and inability to reproduce a recommendation without model access.

“It worked well. I ran into this one issue of memory being the bottleneck.”

said at 03:05

“later when they went through an audit, a third-party vendor dependency was redlined, and then they couldn't go forward.”

said at 04:08
04When to stop renting
Slide at 05:30 · click any slide to enlarge

When to stop renting

  • His framework: rent while pre-product-market-fit and still validating demand, but once post-PMF or once an enterprise has budgeted a project (implying assumed PMF), owning inference infrastructure becomes necessary.
  • He summarizes this as 'rent to learn, own to earn.'

“you can get by by renting. But if you're post-product market fit, you cannot afford to”

said at 04:38

“if you want to learn, you can rent, but if you want to earn, then you have to own.”

said at 07:13
05He open-sourced a cost-optimization tool from the experience
Slide at 06:30 · click any slide to enlarge

He open-sourced a cost-optimization tool from the experience

  • He turned lessons from this process into an open-source project, Just Token Max, which he says he benchmarked against Netflix's Headroom and found superior on multiple parameters.

“We benchmarked against headroom and on many parameters, just token maxes is far superior.”
