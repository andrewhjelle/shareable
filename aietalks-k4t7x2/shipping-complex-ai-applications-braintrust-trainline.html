If you only read one thing

  • The workshop builds a 5-stage agentic support-ticket triage system with tool calling, arguing the real gap in shipping AI isn't model intelligence but operational rigor (tracing, evals, golden datasets) that hasn't kept pace with model capability.
  • Two evaluation types are used: deterministic scoring functions (cheap, unit-test-like) for things you can specify exactly, and LLM-as-judge for nuanced criteria like tone or brand voice that can't be pinned down deterministically.
  • Trainline reports operating at scale (6.3 billion tickets sold, 27 million active users) and Braintrust reports raising $80 million at an $800 million valuation as a nearly 3-year-old company; once confidence is established, the recommended online-scoring sampling rate drops to 5-10% to manage LLM-judge costs.

The real gap isn't model intelligence, it's operational rigor

  • The workshop opens by arguing that current models are already sophisticated enough, but the operational rigor needed to deliver these systems reliably at scale hasn't kept up, which is why proof-of-concept demos routinely fail when moved into production.

03Two types of evaluation: deterministic checks and LLM-as-judge

Two types of evaluation: deterministic checks and LLM-as-judge

  • Evaluations run against a golden dataset use two scoring-function types: deterministic checks (cheap, unit-test-like, no model call) for things that can be specified exactly, and LLM-as-judge for nuanced criteria like brand style or customer satisfaction that can't be captured deterministically.
  • The speakers add that a hand-built test dataset is still no substitute for real-world production data.

“we use kind of two main types of scoring functions. The first of which is deterministic.”

05Cost control: sampling rate and token-latency metrics in production

Cost control: sampling rate and token-latency metrics in production

  • For online scoring, the recommendation is to start with a higher LLM-as-judge sampling rate to establish a baseline, then reduce it to 5-10% to manage cost once confidence is established, while deterministic scores are cheap enough to run on everything.
  • Tracked metrics include time to first token, called out as an important metric customers try to identify.

“you really want to reduce the the sampling rate down to 5 to 10%. So, again, you you're managing your your costs effectively.”
