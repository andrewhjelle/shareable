01Audio understanding as the foundation

Audio understanding as the foundation

  • The speaker frames Gemini 3's audio understanding as the base layer beneath every other audio product: the stated goal is models that deeply comprehend, richly transcribe, and robustly reason through audio, handling mixed languages, dialects, accents, and cross-talk.
  • The EchoScript demo app (built in Google AI Studio on a Gemini 3 Flash preview model) showed this by extracting speaker names, timestamps, language, emotion, and translation from a single API request using a structured response schema.

Speech generation directs a small voice set

  • Unlike TTS providers with large filterable voice libraries, Gemini ships around 30 base voices and instead relies on prompt-based direction (an audio profile, scene, and 'director's note') to make a base voice perform a specific accent or persona, demoed live as an Irish pub voice, a standard American voice, and a Singaporean-accented voice from the same small voice set.

“in in in Gemini, you have, you know, just I think it's like 30-ish sort of base voices”

03Native real-time speech-to-speech
Slide at 11:50 · click any slide to enlarge · 8 more from this section below

Native real-time speech-to-speech

  • Gemini 3.1 Flash Live, launched a couple weeks before the talk, is described as a full-duplex, sound-to-sound real-time multimodal model that ingests text, audio, and video over a websocket connection and returns real-time audio plus a text transcript.
  • The speaker contrasts this with a cascaded pipeline that converts speech to text before running it through a separate LLM, noting reasoning and intelligence are baked directly into the audio model itself, though he cautions that audio benchmarks generally can't be trusted.

“You can ingest text, audio, video in real time through a web socket connection and then you get real-time audio response back as well as kind of text transcript of that”

04Live demo limits and music generation

Live demo limits and music generation

  • The live AI Studio demo (ai.studio/live) can also ingest a webcam or screen feed alongside audio, though screen/video ingestion is capped at a maximum of one frame per second currently.
  • DeepMind also recently released Lyra 3, a music-generation model with lyrics support split into two variants: Lyra 3 Clip for roughly 30-second jingles and Lyra 3 Pro for full-length songs, demoed by having the Live model call Lyra as a tool to generate a requested song live.

“you're basically just ingesting video frames in addition to the audio at a maximum frame rate of one frame per second at the moment”

said at 15:05

“There's a Lyra 3 clip which is a 30-second kind of jingle genera- generation model and then a Lyra 3 Pro is the full-length uh song generation model”

said at 16:18

Commentary (ours)

The most transferable idea for building voice products is treating voice direction like acting notes (audio profile + scene + director's note) fed into a small base-voice set, rather than shopping a large fixed voice catalog -- worth testing against other providers' voice-cloning-heavy approaches for cost and consistency (ours).
