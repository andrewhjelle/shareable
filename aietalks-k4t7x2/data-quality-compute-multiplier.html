Data quality as a compute multiplier

  • The core framing: better data steepens the performance-vs-compute curve, so the same compute budget yields performance equivalent to a much larger one.
  • Morcos stresses there is no single universal dataset, quality is defined relative to the target task/domain, and datasets must be diverse or models become brittle to phrasing changes.

“data quality is a compute multiplier because what it does is it it makes the learning curve steeper”

Curation results on vision-language models

  • Applying DatologyAI's curation pipeline (clean, curate, create, compose) to the Mammoth dataset produced roughly a 14-point absolute improvement on benchmark error over the uncurated input, pushing past the public VLM Pareto frontier and matching a comparable model's performance while using about 145x less training compute.

“you're able to get around a 14 absolute percentage point improvement um holding everything else constant just through better data alone”

04Synthetic rephrasing avoids model collapse
Slide at 12:40 · click any slide to enlarge · 3 more from this section below

Synthetic rephrasing avoids model collapse

  • DatologyAI's synthetic-data approach rewrites source documents into many templated formats (e.g., true/false questions) rather than generating new content from a model's own knowledge.
  • Because the informational content still comes from the source document, Morcos says this sidesteps the model-collapse risk associated with training on model-generated data, while boosting diversity.

“because all the information is coming from the document on the left, you don't have any issue with model collapse”

said at 12:59
05Customer cases: legal mid-training and a $20M frontier-competitive model
Slide at 14:20 · click any slide to enlarge · 6 more from this section below

Customer cases: legal mid-training and a $20M frontier-competitive model

  • For Thomson Reuters, mid-training an open-source model on a mix of curated legal and general data lifted legal-benchmark performance by about 5 percentage points using less than 1% of the pretraining compute budget, without losing general capability.
  • When the same team's existing post-training harness was applied to this mid-trained model instead of the default instruction-tuned one, the measured gain from post-training almost tripled.
  • Separately, RC's Trendy Large model, 17 trillion tokens of curated public data, no proprietary or closed-model data, reached open-frontier-competitive performance for under $20 million total spend.

Commentary (ours)

The headline multiplier numbers (145x, 50x, 3x) are all DatologyAI's own benchmarking of its own product, so they should be read as vendor-reported upper bounds rather than independently replicated results (ours), the underlying mechanism (curation reduces redundant/low-signal tokens) is well-established in the literature Morcos cites, but the specific magnitudes are a sales case study, not a peer-reviewed comparison.
