Documents a concrete integration pattern -- a voice primitive that wraps speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and turn-taking around an already-built chat agent's existing tool calling and evals, rather than requiring a full agent-platform re...

If you only read one thing

  • ElevenLabs' new Voice Engine primitive wraps speech-to-text (Scribe), text-to-speech (V3), and emotion-aware turn-taking around an existing chat agent, because customers like Revolut's customer support had already built and evaluated their own agent and didn't want to rebuild on a full platform.
  • The client SDK adds a voice widget to a site in three lines of code, and a coding-agent skill can convert an existing chat agent into a voice agent in about one prompt.
  • The talk frames voice as more accessible than chat for people who struggle with keyboards or dyslexia, and predicts chat agents will either add voice or die out.

Chat agents in 2025, voice as the next step

  • The speaker frames 2025 as the year chat interfaces became the default way apps added AI.
  • He argues voice is a more natural medium than chat -- quicker, more interactive, and more accessible for people who struggle with keyboards or dyslexia -- and unlocks channels like joining a Zoom call or adding a phone line.

Customers already had an agent and didn't want to rebuild

  • ElevenLabs works with customers like Revolut's customer support.
  • Several of them had already built their own chat agent, including evals and transcription work, and pushed back on replacing it just to add voice, which motivated a wrapper approach instead of a full rebuild.

“we're working powering customers like Revolut's customer support.”

“I've already got my agent. I spent loads of time doing the evals, the transcriptions. Why would I need to completely replace and rebuild with what I have?”

03Voice Engine: wrap voice around an existing agent

Voice Engine: wrap voice around an existing agent

  • The new Voice Engine product, previewed as coming out in a couple of weeks, packages Scribe speech-to-text and V3 text-to-speech with turn-taking as a standalone primitive that wraps around any existing chat agent.
  • The client SDK adds a voice widget to a site in three lines of code.

“we've basically taken this voice engine bit and wrapped it up into its own first class primitive, which makes it really easy for you to add and wrap any existing agent.”

said at 02:34

“We combine the best models, so speech to text with scribe, which is the most accurate model, as well as the text to speech models like V3.”

said at 03:06

“It's basically three lines you can then add and you have a widget in your site.”
