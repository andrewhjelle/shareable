How Google DeepMind is researching the next Frontier of AI for Gemini

If you only read one thing

  • Gemini Embeddings 2 is fully omnimodal (one vector for up to 8K tokens of text, 128 seconds of video, 80 seconds of audio, or a full PDF) and uses Matryoshka representation learning so retrieval can start at 256 dimensions and expand as needed.
  • DeepMind's weather models beat physics-based baselines: GraphCast predicted Hurricane Lee's landfall accurately 9 days out versus 6 days for the best physics models, and GenCast was more accurate than 1,300 gold-standard forecasts 97% of the time while producing a 15-day forecast in 8 minutes on a single chip.
  • Genie's world models can now generate interactive environments with persistent memory -- Hadsell demos running away from and back to a starting point in a Genie-generated world and finding everything unchanged.

01Embedding models as generative AI's retrieval companion

Embedding models as generative AI's retrieval companion

  • Hadsell frames embedding models as a critical companion to generative AI for retrieval and comparison tasks, drawing an analogy to neuroscience's 'Jennifer Aniston cell' concept.
  • Gemini Embeddings 2, released recently, is derived from Gemini and is fully omnimodal, producing a single vector from text, video, audio, or PDF input.
  • It uses Matryoshka representation learning (MRL), letting the same network represent embeddings at different dimensionalities so retrieval can start small and expand for more expressiveness.

02GraphCast: a graph neural network beating physics-based weather models

GraphCast: a graph neural network beating physics-based weather models

  • GraphCast, developed with the UK Met Office, predicts the state of the atmosphere up to 15 days out globally across roughly 100 variables using a spherical graph neural network trained on 40 years of global weather data.
  • On Hurricane Lee's 2024 landfall in Nova Scotia, GraphCast's prediction of the landfall location was accurate 9 days out, compared to 6 days for the best physics-based gold-standard models.

03GenCast and FGN: probabilistic forecasting, faster and more accurate

GenCast and FGN: probabilistic forecasting, faster and more accurate

  • GenCast, the follow-up model, is probabilistic rather than deterministic, which Hadsell says better captures weather's chaotic tail behavior.
  • Benchmarked against 1,300 gold-standard forecasts, GenCast was more accurate 97% of the time, and could produce a 15-day forecast in 8 minutes on a single chip instead of hours on a large supercomputer.
  • The newest model, FGN (functional generative network), predicts cyclones directly rather than as post-processing, and is already in use at the US National Hurricane Center.

04Genie: world models with persistent memory

Genie: world models with persistent memory

  • Hadsell traces DeepMind's world-model work from Genie 1 (a few-second 2D platformer) through Genie 2 (interactive but not real-time 3D) to the latest version, which generates real-time, embodied, interactive 3D environments from a prompt or a video fragment.
  • She demonstrates that these environments retain memory: running away from a starting point and back, everything remains exactly as it was.

“I can spend a minute running in one direction, run back to the start and everything is exactly as it was at the beginning because we have a really good memory”

said at 18:19
05Where this could go: worlds as entertainment and education

Where this could go: worlds as entertainment and education

  • Hadsell closes by speculating about adversarially reprompting someone else's world in real time as a new entertainment format, and about using generative, persistent worlds as a way to let people learn about a place or topic by going into it directly.

“the ability to be able to go into a world in order to learn about it, I think is incredibly powerful”
