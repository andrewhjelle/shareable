02Standard training: align to an external image encoder

Standard training: align to an external image encoder

  • Generative models trained by denoising noisy images don't inherently learn physical constraints, so BFL aligns training to an external, separately-trained encoder (representation alignment), which the speaker says converges 70 times faster.
  • The tradeoff is a scaling ceiling, since the frozen external encoder can't scale with the generative model, and a need for a separate encoder per modality; switching to the technically better DINOv3 encoder for alignment actually made results worse than DINOv2.

“it is 70 times faster to actually converge and to reduce the loss when you use this external alignment.”

said at 08:44

“You're still like limited by this encoder that you have on the side, you know? You never actually scaling up fully with that.”

said at 09:16

“DinoV3 is a better model technically, per se, than DinoV2. But when you train your model, actually getting worse performances, you know, DinoV3 is here in right in red and green.”

said at 10:17
03Self Flow: dropping the external encoder

Self Flow: dropping the external encoder

  • BFL's Self Flow research paper, released about a month and a half before the talk, combines representation learning and generation in the same self-supervised flow without any external encoder.
  • The team reports it beats a flow-matching baseline on audio, image, and video modalities.

“we combine representation learning and generation in the same flow. And you see here on the left, you have videos, images, or audio.”

said at 11:20

“we're comparing flow matching, which is the usual way of training models, with ours. And you can see we are better in audio.”

Klein: pushing toward real-time generation
