  • In a 3-month embedded customer engagement, Cognition says it delivered work equivalent to roughly 150% additional headcount, while compressing delivery timelines by about 82% and roughly doubling PR throughput versus prior single-point tools (c3, c4, c5).

Coding is mostly solved; delivery is the remaining problem

  • Wu says Cognition shipped almost an order of magnitude more good-quality, robust PRs across the organization over the last 6 months using its own agent, despite being behind on hiring.
  • She argues writing code faster is only about 20% of the actual problem — testing, review, deployment, and maintenance across an enterprise make up the rest.

Faster delivery timelines and higher PR throughput

Public case studies: Nubank, a Latin American bank, and Built

  • Wu names three public customer examples: an ETL migration at Nubank originally staffed with 50 engineers, delivered in roughly a third of the planned timeline; a tax identification system migration at one of Latin America's largest banks, delivered at about half the expected effort; and Built, where Cognition claims to generate the weekly engineering output of more than 10 additional engineers.

“there was an ETL migration. They had 50 engineers staffing this migration. We were able to deliver this within, um, I think like 1/3 of the timeline.”
