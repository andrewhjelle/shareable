How to Leverage Domain Expertise

SpeakerChris Lovejoy · Notius Labs Watchyoutu.be/kfSDc2eVLo4
Vertical AIEvals & benchmarks

If you only read one thing

  • Chris Lovejoy argues winning in vertical AI is an organizational problem rather than a model-quality problem, citing Gartner's finding that about 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned last year.
  • He frames three ways to bake in domain expertise: oracle (the expert directly edits outputs, as at Granola), evaluator (the expert defines quality metrics), and architect (the expert designs a self-improving system, the direction Anterior evolved toward).

01Why vertical AI is an org problem, not a model problem

Lovejoy states his belief that winning in vertical AI is fundamentally an organizational problem rather than a matter of picking the best model. He cites a Gartner figure that about 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned last year, and attributes much of that to companies building AI systems without a deep understanding of the workflows they're automating.

“I think that fundamentally uh winning in vertical AI is an organizational problem.”

02Three models: oracle, evaluator, architect

He lays out three ways a domain expert can be embedded in an organization. As an oracle, the expert directly edits AI outputs and prompts themselves. As an evaluator, the expert instead defines how quality is measured and feeds that data to engineers. As an architect, the expert designs a system that improves itself automatically from usage, with less human-in-the-loop involvement.

“The first of these I call the oracle which is that you have your domain expert who directly embeds their domain expertise into your actual application.”

03Case studies: Granola and Anterior

At Granola, an AI meeting-notes company that recently passed a billion-dollar valuation, the first employee has remained the primary oracle for AI quality because there's no single objectively correct meeting note. At Anterior, a prior-authorization company where Lovejoy was first technical employee, the domain-expert role progressed from oracle to evaluator to architect as the AI's outputs became a clearly measurable correct/incorrect decision requiring an adaptive system.

“generates AI meeting notes, and recently uh kind of passed a billion in valuation”

04How to leverage the domain expert you hire

Lovejoy recommends naming a single principal domain expert who is accountable for AI quality, rather than splitting responsibility across a committee. He describes a failure mode at one company where two senior clinicians shared the role without clear ownership, progress on the AI-quality system was slow, and both left the company within 12-18 months.

“there's a lot of value organizationally in defining a principal domain expert”
