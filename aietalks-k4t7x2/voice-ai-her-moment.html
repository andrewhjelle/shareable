01Full duplex vs. half duplex: why most voice agents still can't overlap speech

Full duplex vs. half duplex: why most voice agents still can't overlap speech

  • The speaker says Moshi remains the only full duplex voice model.
  • Most speech-to-speech models, including what he identifies as OpenAI's advanced voice mode and Sesame AI's model, are half duplex -- the model is either listening or speaking and can't handle the ambiguity of overlapping human speech, coughs, or back-channeling.
  • In some cultures, he says, up to 20% of conversation time is overlapped between speakers.

02The bottleneck moved from TTS latency to tool calls

The bottleneck moved from TTS latency to tool calls

  • Teams are fighting to shave 10-20 milliseconds off TTS latency while a tool call or router adds 500 milliseconds to several seconds of latency on top.
  • The speaker argues the tool call itself, being unpredictable, is now the main bottleneck rather than the voice model.
