If you only read one thing

  • AI Engineer's 9-person team (running a business over $9M) hooks Devin into non-coding work -- a pixel-perfect Figma-to-website build, conference logistics via email, even researching where to buy a lobster prop -- and expects to manage 6,000 attendees in San Francisco this summer at the same headcount.
  • A non-technical designer independently started directing Devin with red-line canvas annotations, the same workflow tldraw's Steve Ruiz uses, without ever being taught to do it.
