01Fiscal 2026 results and market share

e.l.f. Beauty reported 25% net sales growth for fiscal 2026 and its 29th consecutive quarter of net sales growth in Q4. The ELF Cosmetics brand gained 115 basis points of US market share, the largest gain among nearly 1,000 tracked cosmetics brands, extending a streak of 29 straight quarters of share gains.

“In fiscal 26, we grew net sales 25% and adjusted IBITA 13%. Q4 marked our 29th consecutive quarter of net sales growth.”

02Rhode and international expansion

Rhode, acquired in August 2025, delivered over $500 million in global retail sales on an annualized basis and grew net sales more than 80% year-over-year, while still in under 20% of one major retail partner's doors. International net sales grew 38% for the year as the company launched with eight new international retail customers across 14 countries.

“On an annualized basis in fiscal 26, road delivered over $500 million in global retail sales and approximately $390 million in net sales, growing net sales over 80% year-over-year.”

03Core brand slowdown and a pricing response

Management said ELF brand consumption moderated from high single digits in fiscal 2026 to low single digits in the last 12 weeks, attributed partly to weaker spring innovation. In response, the company tested cutting the Halo Glow Skin Tint price from $18 to $14 and reported a 38% unit lift on Amazon and a 36% lift across all retailers.

“ELF brands global consumption has moderated from high single digits in fiscal 26 to low single digits in the last 12 weeks.”

04Balance sheet and fiscal 2027 outlook

The company ended fiscal 2026 with $290 million cash on hand and had repurchased approximately $50 million of stock during the year. Fiscal 2027 guidance calls for net sales growth of approximately 12 to 14%, with Q1 organic net sales expected down high single digits due to a shipping-period year-over-year comparison.

“For the full year, we expect net sales growth of approximately 12 to 14%.”
