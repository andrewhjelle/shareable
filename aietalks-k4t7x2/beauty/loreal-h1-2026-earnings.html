01First-half results

The CFO reported first-half 2026 like-for-like sales growth of 6.5%, a gross margin of 74.8%, and a first-half-record operating margin of 21.3%, with operating net cash flow up 13.9% year on year. E-commerce grew 18% (almost twice the market rate) to 7.4 billion euros, outpacing 2.5% growth in physical retail.
