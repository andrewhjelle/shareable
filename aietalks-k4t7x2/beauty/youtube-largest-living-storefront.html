Why YouTube Is Now the World's Largest Living Storefront

SpeakerCEW Future Beauty Summit · Google / YouTube Watchyoutu.be/olf1rAC_Np8
Creators & influencersSocial commerce & TikTok

If you only read one thing

  • YouTube is cited as the top-referenced platform for generative engine optimization (GEO), and 35 billion hours of shopping content were watched on it last year
  • Gen Z and Millennials say they trust YouTubers more than musicians, athletes, or actors
  • Product reviews run 30-90 seconds on TikTok but 8-15 minutes on YouTube, and that extra time is framed as tracking with purchase intent
01GEO means showing up on YouTube

The speaker cites an eMarketer article naming Google/YouTube as the top-referenced platform for generative engine optimization (GEO) queries. The recommended tactics are concise, simple messaging and working with creators who are authentic and clearly articulate information, since that builds credibility with the large language models doing the scraping.

“Google YouTube is now the top referenced channel platform in terms of GEO”

02Product design and scent build loyalty

The talk argues beauty's emotional pull, meaning design, scent, and texture, is a real differentiator. Internal analysis of hero products is described as showing a direct correlation between those sensory attributes and deep consumer loyalty.

“actually has a direct correlated correlation between design, scent, texture with really deep consumer loyalty”

▶ watch at 01:01
03Creators are trusted more than celebrities

Gen Z and Millennial audiences are described as trusting YouTubers over musicians, athletes, and actors, attributed to the hours viewers spend with long- and short-form creator content. A Bella Hadid comment on a product is offered as an example of the authenticity signal that builds this trust.

“Gen Z and Millennials trust YouTubers. In fact, they trust them more than musicians, athletes, actors.”

“I want to just highlight here this comment about Bella Hadid, who's talking about our products. And it shows I can trust that she genuinely uses these products.”

04Longer content signals purchase intent

The speaker contrasts short-form and long-form video: a product review runs 30-90 seconds on TikTok versus 8-15 minutes on YouTube. The claim is that this extra time spent with content tracks with higher consideration and purchase intent.

“the average length of a product review on TikTok is 30 to 90 seconds”

▶ watch at 03:10
05YouTube as a storefront

YouTube is described as no longer just a video platform but 'the world's largest living storefront,' citing 35 billion hours of shopping content consumed on the platform last year. The talk frames 2026 as the year YouTube adds its own shopping formats to match Instagram and TikTok.

“YouTube is no longer just a video platform. It's actually the world's largest living storefront.”
