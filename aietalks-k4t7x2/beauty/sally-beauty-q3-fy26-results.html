01Quarterly numbers and guidance

The CEO reported fiscal Q3 net sales of $935 million with flat comparable sales, adjusted operating income of $87 million, and adjusted diluted EPS of 55 cents, up 8% year over year. For the full year, the company narrowed guidance to comparable sales of approximately 0.5% and adjusted operating earnings of $329 to $335 million.

“Fiscal Q3 net sales totaled $935 million with comparable sales flat. Gross margin expansion and ongoing cost management translated to strong bottom line delivery.”

02Hair care is lapping last year's launch

The CFO said BSG (the professional/salon-distribution segment) hair care sales were pressured this quarter by lapping the April 2025 launch of K18, even as stylist sentiment and color/nail services stayed healthy. Management said absolute purchase levels have not declined even as shoppers get more choiceful about where they spend.

“In the quarter, we lapped the April 2025 launch of K18, pressuring the flat hair care sales trend of the last few quarters.”

“The consumer has remained extremely resilient. You know, so when we see absolute purchase levels, we are not seeing those decline.”

03Store remodels and cost-cutting

The company said its Sally Ignited store-remodel program had completed 33 refreshes with 17 more planned for Q4, on track for 80 remodeled locations by fiscal year-end. Separately, its multi-year 'Fuel for Growth' cost program is on track to deliver $45 million in FY2026 savings toward a cumulative $120 million target.

“As of the end of July, we have completed 33 store refreshes year to date and we have another 17 planned for fiscal Q4.”

“We are tracking to our plan to generate $45 million of benefits in fiscal 2026 and will have captured about 120 million of cumulative run rate savings over a three-year period”

04How shoppers are buying: price-first, pickup-in-store

Executives said shoppers have shifted from stacking percentage-off promotions to wanting a clear, upfront price, a shift management says is shaping how it communicates pricing rather than how much it discounts. Buy-online-pickup-in-store made up the majority of app order volume, which the company called its most efficient delivery channel, as average order value rose 6%.

“last year they migrated much more to saying I don't want more out of pocket. What I really want to do is get a great price on my item”
