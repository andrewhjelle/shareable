01Anthropic and the Pentagon standoff

The Pentagon (referred to on-air as the War Department) asked Anthropic to remove AI safeguards restricting its models' use for surveillance and autonomous weapons, and Dario Amodei refused. The hosts cite Washington Post reporting that the dispute reduces to a scenario question -- whether the Pentagon could use Anthropic's models against inbound nuclear missiles -- to which Dario reportedly responded that Anthropic would need to be consulted first. Sam Altman is shown saying he doesn't personally think the Pentagon should threaten the Defense Production Act against Anthropic.

02Anthropic's revenue growth vs. OpenAI

The hosts display a chart showing Anthropic's revenue growing roughly 10x per year against OpenAI's 3.4x, with a crossover point placed in the middle of this year. Dave Blundin says every company he's involved with defaults to Claude for corporate and internal work. Alex Wissner-Gross argues the real split isn't chatbots versus agents but consumer versus enterprise, tracing it to Anthropic's compute scarcity forcing an early focus on code generation and enterprise use cases.

“the slope of the line for that purple line is OpenAI. It's 3.4X increase per year. While uh Anthropic is growing in terms of revenues at 10X per year”

03Consulting and audit firms under AI pressure

Discussing Accenture linking employee promotions to AI tool usage, Salim Ismail argues consulting firms face a growth opportunity because their clients are further behind and need more help, calling institutional rebuilding around AI 'the biggest advisory opportunity in the history of mankind.' Peter Diamandis warns that measuring AI usage rather than output quality risks Goodhart's law. Dave Blundin adds that audit firms aren't disappearing but are cutting headcount by 80-90% for the same volume of work as financial systems move toward real-time self-auditing.

“is we need to rebuild every institution and re-architect every institution by which we run the world. And that is the biggest advisory opportunity in the history of mankind”
