How Anthropic's product team moves faster than anyone else | Cat Wu (Head of Product, Claude Code)

If you only read one thing

  • Anthropic's Claude Code team ships almost all features under a 'research preview' label specifically to lower the commitment bar and get things out in a week or two (c3)

01Faster shipping cycles and the PM/eng split

Wu says product feature timelines at Anthropic have compressed from 6 months down to 1 month and sometimes a single day. She describes her working relationship with Claude Code tech lead Boris as roughly 80% shared judgment, with each of them independently driving the remaining 20% they individually care most about. To keep shipping fast, the team brands almost all its releases as 'research preview,' which lowers the bar for what's required before something goes out.

“The timelines for a lot of our product features have gone down from 6 months to 1 month and sometimes to even 1 day.”

02What's actually driving the speed: process, not just Mythos

Asked whether internal access to the still-unreleased Mythos model explains the team's shipping pace, Wu says the team has been moving fast for several quarters already and that Mythos accounts for only a small part of the increase, with process and team expectations doing most of the work.

“We've been moving pretty fast for several quarters now, so I think it it's not fully Mythos.”

▶ watch at 10:48
03The Claude Code leak and the OpenClaw access decision

Wu attributes the earlier Claude Code source code leak to human error in a PR that updated the package release process, noting it passed through two layers of human review before the mistake got out. Separately, she confirms Anthropic made a deliberate decision to prioritize Claude subscription access for first-party products and the API over third-party tools like OpenClaw.

“There was um a human working with Claude to write a PR. This was just an update to how we release our packages. And it actually went through two layers of human review.”

▶ watch at 12:20

“we we did have to make the hard decision that we needed to prioritize our first-party products and our API.”

04Growth numbers and token economics

Wu cites Anthropic's revenue growth as roughly $11 billion in ARR added in a single month, calling the pace unlike anything she's seen. On token spend, she says the token cost per engineer or knowledge worker rises with every model jump or major product improvement, though it remains well below average engineer salary for now.

“like 11 billion dollars in ARR in one month. Drips and growth.”

05Building for the current model: the to-do-list story and the 100% rule

Wu describes how Claude Code originally struggled to complete large multi-file refactors, prompting the team to add an explicit to-do-list feature so the model wouldn't stop partway through; later models internalized this behavior without needing the scaffold. She also advises listeners building their own automations to push past 90-95% accuracy to 100%, arguing that anything less isn't a real automation.

“people would ask it to do these large refactors and Quad Code would say, "Okay, cool. I need to change these like 20 call sites." And it would go and change five of them and then stop.”

▶ watch at 61:21

“I would also push listeners towards focusing on bringing your automations from, "Okay, this is a cool concept." to like, "Hey, this actually works 100% of the time."”
