The $285B Sell-Off Was Just the Beginning — The Infrastructure Story Is Bigger.

SpeakerNate B Jones · Nate B Jones Watchyoutu.be/O-0poNv2jD4
AgentsMarkets & economyAI industry

If you only read one thing

  • Coinbase, Stripe, Cloudflare, and Exa are independently building agent-native payment, content, and search infrastructure within the same few-month window
  • Agent-native search providers beat Google-wrapping tools on latency (Brave at 669 milliseconds vs. Parallel Pro at 13.6 seconds) and accuracy (Exa scores 95% on SimpleQA)
  • OpenAI's skills/shell/compaction toolkit already shows a measured gain (Glean's Salesforce task accuracy rose from 73% to 85%), while PolyMarket bots and an OpenClaw-chained product-video demo show agents beginning to transact and produce work on their own, with every serious security approach now treating the agent itself as untrusted
01Money and content are being rebuilt for agents, not humans

Coinbase's Agentic Wallets use the X42 protocol, already reported to have processed over 50 million machine-to-machine transactions. Stripe had to rebuild its Radar fraud-detection model from scratch because its old signals were calibrated for human shopping behavior, not software buyers. Cloudflare, which the narrator says serves roughly 20% of the web, now converts pages to markdown on request for agents rather than requiring third-party scraping.

03OpenAI's skills/shell/compaction toolkit in production

OpenAI's skills, shell tool, and compaction features let agents load versioned instruction bundles, run real terminal environments, and automatically manage long context windows. Glean, an early skills customer, saw accuracy on Salesforce-related tasks jump from 73% to 85% after adopting a single structured skill.

“Glean is an enterprise search company and it was an early skills customer and they saw accuracy on Salesforce related tasks jump from 73 to 85%”

04Agents already transacting, and the security response to that

PolyMarket processed $12 billion in volume in January 2026 alone; researchers found algorithmic traders extracted roughly $40 million in arbitrage profits over 86 million bets in a 12-month period. A developer demo chained OpenClaw, a video model, and Chatcut to turn an Amazon product link into a finished product video with no human touching any step. In response, security approaches from Ironclaw, OpenAI's shell tool, and Coinbase's wallets all treat the agent itself as a potential adversary rather than a trusted employee.
