04Multi-sensory AI and the coming compute crunch

The panel connects Apple's patent for cameras in AirPods to a real-time multi-sensory model from Thinking Machines, describing a model that watches a user's desktop and webcam and listens to voices, uploading to two different models (one fast, one deep-thinking) every 200 milliseconds. Jason argues this always-on, non-turn-based querying pattern would require a hardware upgrade to the average desktop if used for eight hours a day.

05El Niño's economic ripple effects

Freiberg's science-corner segment presents a forecast that record-high ocean heat -- about 11 million extra terawatt-hours of stored energy, against the planet's 25,000 terawatt-hour annual usage -- points to a record-breaking El Niño with major second-order effects: atmospheric river flooding in the US Southwest, heat waves in Canada, and crop-failure risk in Brazil, Australia, and India's monsoon-dependent farming.
