The New Jobs AI Will Create

SpeakerNathaniel Whittemore · The AI Daily Brief Watchyoutu.be/WhAAKxPlMhw
AI industryMarkets & economy

If you only read one thing

  • AI-jobs predictions typically assume labor demand stays fixed (the lump-of-labor fallacy); NLW argues demand instead expands through elasticities like price, access, complexity, continuity, personalization, and relational value
  • Even where AGI can perform a task, NLW argues it doesn't automatically erase demand for what he calls the 'human premium' -- relationship, presence, trust, accountability, translation, behavior change, and provenance
  • Applying this to healthcare, he projects a 'continuous care navigator' role alone could represent 276,000 to 1.2 million new US jobs depending on enrollment scale
01The hidden assumption in AI job-loss predictions

NLW argues most AI-jobs analysis treats AI purely as an increase in labor supply and assumes demand for goods and services stays constant, which he calls a version of the lump-of-labor fallacy. He reframes the AGI objection from a capability question -- can AI perform the task -- to a service-design question: does AI-only delivery actually satisfy the demand.

“AI is mostly analyzed right now as a labor supply story. In other words, AI increases the supply of labor, so labor gets cheaper and workers get displaced”

“we think about the question of jobs only as a question of can AI perform the task? It's a capability question that assumes labor demand is grounded entirely and only in what humans can do”

02The human premium: seven reasons AGI doesn't erase demand

NLW defines the 'human premium' as the portion of economic value that stays attached to human involvement even when AI can perform the underlying task. He names seven categories -- relationship, embodied presence, trust, accountability, translation, behavior change, and provenance/status -- and details accountability as wanting someone who signs off, escalates, explains, and is responsible when something goes wrong.

“while AGI can eat tasks and will eat many tasks, it will not automatically eat that demand for things like trust and accountability”

▶ watch at 11:41

“the fourth category of human premium, which is accountability, where the demand constraint is someone has to own this. People want a person who signs off, escalates, explains, and is responsible when things go wrong”

▶ watch at 13:44
03Where new demand comes from: affordability and possibility unlocks

NLW describes an 'affordability unlock,' where AI makes an existing service cheap enough for new buyers, using small businesses that currently can't afford a $5,000 design project, $3,000 marketing campaign, $2,000 legal review, or $1,500 analytics report. He contrasts this with a 'possibility unlock,' where an entirely new service model becomes operationally viable, illustrated by continuous preventative healthcare that most people, unlike Bryan Johnson, don't currently have access to.

“the average small business owner might not have the budget to buy a $5,000 design project, a $3,000 marketing campaign, a $2,000 legal review, and a $1,500 analytics report”

▶ watch at 07:04

“The vast majority of people not named Bryan Johnson don't currently have someone continuously watching their health data or tracking their care plan or noticing drift or coordinating with their pharmacy”

▶ watch at 08:37
04Healthcare case study: sizing the new jobs

Assuming one navigator can handle 100-150 patients, NLW projects a 'continuous care navigator' role could represent between 276,000 and 1.2 million US jobs depending on whether 40 million or 120 million adults enroll in continuous care. He concludes that even under an AGI scenario, the expanded demand created by lower AI-driven costs means more human work overall, not less.

“you're talking about about 276,000 jobs. On the other end of the spectrum, where we've got 120 million enrolled patients, and we're assuming that they can only handle 100 people each, you're talking about 1.2 million jobs”

▶ watch at 19:58

“And more demand, even in a scenario of AGI, means more human work ahead, too”
