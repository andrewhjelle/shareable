01The job-apocalypse narrative gets pushback

Ezra Klein published a piece arguing the AI job apocalypse probably won't happen the way the most fearful predictions suggest, inspired by economist Alex Em's essay on the 'relational sector.' Separately, a16z's David George published a chart-heavy piece arguing the same, citing over a century of employment data and noting that mentions of AI workforce impact on public earnings calls favor augmentation over substitution.

“the AI job apocalypse probably won't happen, at least in the way that the most fearful folks have been suggesting for some time now.”

02Wall Street stops pricing AI as a bubble

Anthropic and OpenAI both launched enterprise deployment joint ventures this week, with OpenAI's structured around a $10 billion starting valuation and $4 billion investment, and Anthropic's around a $1.5 billion investment. JP Morgan's Jamie Dimon and BlackRock's Larry Fink both publicly affirmed the AI investment boom is real rather than a bubble, and The Information reported the Google-Anthropic compute deal is worth $200 billion.

“And when I say massive, I'm talking about a $10 billion starting valuation and a $4 billion investment for OpenAI and a $1.5 billion investment from Anthropic.”

03Compute infrastructure bets: SpaceX, Terafab, Corning

Anthropic and Elon Musk announced a new partnership under which Anthropic takes over the entire capacity of xAI's Colossus 1 data center. Separately, a legal filing revealed Elon's Terafab chip manufacturing project in Texas is now projected to cost $55-119 billion, well above earlier estimates of $20-25 billion, and Nvidia announced a partnership with Corning, which holds more than 70% of the fiber-optic glass market used in data center networking.

04Layoffs blamed on AI don't all hold up, and Codex ships /goal

The host flags that Cloudflare laid off 1,100 people despite having hired 2,000 just months earlier, complicating the media narrative that AI was the primary driver of its layoffs. Separately, Codex shipped a new /goal feature that lets an agent keep working autonomously on a stated goal across turns until it's complete, described internally as OpenAI's take on the 'Ralph loop.'
