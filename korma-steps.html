This is not butter chicken

The instinct will be to reach for the butter chicken shelf. Don’t. Two ingredients from that recipe will collapse this one back into a paler version of a dish you already cook.

  • No tomato. None. Not crushed, not paste. The body comes from ground cashews and deeply browned onions instead.
  • No kashmiri chili. The warmth is white pepper, about ¼ tsp. That is the entire heat of the dish.
  • Whole spices, bloomed in ghee first — cardamom pods, cinnamon stick, cloves, bay. Not ground masala stirred in late.
  • The onions carry it. 15–20 minutes to deep golden brown, the color of a roasted peanut. Highest-leverage step in the recipe and the easiest one to cut short.
  • Finishes pale ivory, not orange-red. If it looks like butter chicken, something went in that shouldn’t have.

Mild by design. There is no chili anywhere in this, so no split pot and no kid-mild half. Adults add heat at the table.

              Chicken korma

              1. Marinate. Cut 2 lb boneless skinless thighs into 1½-inch pieces. Whisk ¾ cup whole-milk yogurt with 1 tbsp grated ginger, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tsp kosher salt. Coat the chicken. Fridge 1–24 hrs.
              2. Birista. Slice 3 yellow onions thin. Fry in ½ cup neutral oil over medium, 15–20 min, until deep golden brown. Drain on paper towel. Reserve 2 tbsp for garnish; blend the rest with ¼ cup water into a paste.
              3. Cashew paste. Soak ½ cup raw cashews in ½ cup hot water for 20 min. Blend completely smooth.
              4. Bloom whole spices. 3 tbsp ghee in a heavy pot over medium. Add 4 cracked green cardamom pods, 1 two-inch cinnamon stick, 4 cloves, 2 bay leaves. 45 sec.
              5. Onion paste in, cook 3–4 min. Then 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp ground cumin, ¼ tsp white pepper, 30 sec.
              6. Chicken in. Pull the pot off the heat 30 sec first. Add the chicken with all its marinade yogurt. Back on medium, stirring constantly 2–3 min. Then cover, low, 15–18 min.
              7. Thicken. Stir in the cashew paste + ½ cup water. Simmer uncovered 5–8 min until it coats a spoon.
              8. Finish off the heat. ¼ cup heavy cream, ½ tsp garam masala, ⅛ tsp mace, and the saffron if using (15 threads steeped 10 min in 2 tbsp warm milk). Salt to taste.
              9. Rest 10 min before serving. It thickens and settles. Garnish with the reserved fried onions + slivered almonds.
              10. Serve with basmati and naan.

              The three ways this goes wrong

              1. Split yogurt. Cold yogurt into a hot pan curdles into grainy threads. Yogurt at room temp, pot off the heat when it goes in, constant stirring for two minutes. If it splits anyway, the cashew paste at step 7 largely covers it.
              2. Under-browned onions. Pale onions give you a flat, thin korma with nothing underneath. Twenty minutes is the real number.
              3. Boiling after the cream. Cream goes in off the heat and never comes back to a boil.

              Kid notes

              Already mild — no chili in the dish at all. Drop the white pepper to ⅛ tsp for a first run with Zada. It is sweet-leaning and creamy, the same profile both kids accepted in butter chicken. If it reads bland to adults, the fix is salt and a squeeze of lemon, not chili.
