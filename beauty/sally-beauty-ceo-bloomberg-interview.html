If you only read one thing

  • Sally Beauty stock sold off sharply intraday, its steepest drop since May 2023, after weak Q3 guidance citing pressure in low-income stores, even though Q2 comps were positive at 1.3% (c1, c2).
  • CEO Denise Paulonis said lower-middle-income customers are trading toward at-home color and nail care rather than salon visits, with US color up 12% even as discretionary tool purchases soften (c3, c4).
  • E-commerce is up 28% with a new TikTok Shop launch, and about 75% of marketplace-driven transactions (DoorDash-type) are estimated to be incremental new business (c5, c6, c7).
01Stock reaction and Q2 results

Stock reaction and Q2 results

  • Sally Beauty shares dropped sharply intraday after the company gave a weak Q3 outlook citing pressure in stores that identify as low income.
  • This came despite a Q2 that Paulonis called strong: overall sales up 2.3%, comparable sales up 1.3%, and the US Sally brand up 4.4%.

“The company gave a weak outlook for the third quarter and on a conference call, the company said it's seeing pressure in stores that identify as low income and frugality among lower middle income consumers.”

said at 09:15

“our overall sales were up 2.3% comp sales up 1.3% and real outperformance on the consumer side with our Sally brand up 4.4% here in the US.”

said at 10:18
02Consumer trade-down: home over salon

Consumer trade-down: home over salon

  • Paulonis described the lower-middle-income customer as more choiceful, skipping replacement of discretionary tools like blow dryers if not needed, while still buying hair color, nail gel, and press-on nails.
  • She said customers are shifting some salon coloring visits to at-home coloring, with the US color business up 12%.

“we do serve a lower middle income consumer. And so, we watch them be more choiceful in their behavior, which means they don't replace things like blow dryers or flat irons if they don't need them.”

said at 10:18

“for those folks who color their hair, a lot of them share between coloring in a salon and coloring at home. Our color business was up 12% in the US.”

said at 11:50
03Digital and marketplace growth

Digital and marketplace growth

  • Sally's US e-commerce business is up 28%, supported by a newly launched presence on TikTok Shop, which Paulonis framed as important because that is where beauty customers are engaging.
  • She also cited internal analysis suggesting about 75% of transactions coming through delivery marketplaces like DoorDash are incremental new business rather than cannibalizing existing sales.

“our e-commerce business is up 28% in the Sally business in the US.”

said at 15:29

“We just recently launched on TikTok Shop, a really important place to be cuz that's where customers are and that's where they're engaging with beauty.”

said at 16:00

“we've seen about 75% of those transactions through places like DoorDash be incremental business to us, which we think is great news and is bringing a new customer into the Sally fold.”

said at 16:31
04Capital allocation and labor

Capital allocation and labor

  • Sally Beauty is targeting a net leverage ratio of 1.5 to 2 and is currently at 1.5, continuing modest debt paydown.
  • On staffing, Paulonis said stores and distribution centers are able to hire as needed and that voluntary turnover has been declining.

“So, we have a targeted net leverage ratio of 1.5 to 2. We're at the 1.5 level. So, we're still doing a little bit of paydown.”

said at 18:05

“out in the field in both our stores and our distribution centers, we are able to hire as we need to. We've actually seen turnover slow.”

said at 18:37
