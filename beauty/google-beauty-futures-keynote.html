01AI is changing how people decide

AI is changing how people decide

  • The talk cites Google data that 2 billion people use AI Overviews daily, powered by Gemini.
  • Among AI Overview/AI Mode users, 79% report making faster decisions and also making more confident ones, and Google's CEO said Q4 2025 search volume was the highest on record, countering 2024 predictions that chatbots would replace search.

02Shoppers are less loyal to brands and retailers

Shoppers are less loyal to brands and retailers

  • Consumers are described as coming to Google increasingly without a specific brand in mind.
  • In the UK, 72% of shoppers say they're open to trying a different brand and a similar share is open to shopping at a different retailer, framed as both a risk and an opportunity to intercept shoppers at the point of comparison.

03Dupes are going mainstream

Dupes are going mainstream

  • 63% of consumers say they no longer believe premium products outperform mass-market ones, driving a 'propensity towards dupes' that some shoppers see as a response to an insult-tax on high prices.
  • Dupes are forecast to grow from 4.5% of the market in 2020 to 8.9% by 2030, most pronounced in fragrance.

04Agentic checkout is already live in the US

Agentic checkout is already live in the US

  • Google's Universal Commerce Protocol, launched in the US in January, already lets shoppers complete a full purchase inside Gemini with retailers such as Etsy and Wayfair, with a UK launch expected in 2026.
  • Separately, online beauty retail is cited as the fastest-growing distribution channel across Europe at a 5.5% CAGR.
