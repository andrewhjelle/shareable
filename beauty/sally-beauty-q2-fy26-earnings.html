01Q2 results: color leads growth

Q2 results: color leads growth

  • Sally Beauty delivered adjusted operating income of $73 million and adjusted diluted EPS of $0.44, above its guidance range.
  • The color category drove segment performance, up 11% company-wide and 12% at Sally US and Canada, and the Sally segment's net sales rose 4.1% to $521 million.

“Strong gross margins and effective cost control enabled us to deliver bottomline results above our guidance range with adjusted operating income coming in at $73 million and adjusted diluted EPS of 44.”

02New categories and channels

New categories and channels

  • Fragrance expanded from roughly 1,000 to 2,000 Sally US stores during the quarter and is running ahead of expectations.
  • The company launched Sally Beauty on TikTok Shop in March, featuring its owned-brand portfolio plus an initial set of national brands, and entered the skin and spa category in its BSG segment with two brands across 250 stores, with plans to add another 250 stores in Q4.

BSG segment: flat sales, better margins

  • The BSG professional segment posted roughly flat topline results but improved operating margin by 90 basis points to 12.4%, reflecting cost discipline rather than sales growth.

Cost savings and updated guidance
