Marketing spend and the indie-vs-major divide

  • Henanus says beauty likely spends more on marketing than any other industry, citing figures from roughly 35% up to 50% of retail revenue.
  • Stingler contrasts her early Neutrogena years, when major brands dominated on ad budget, with today, when she says indie brands are outpacing majors on marketing innovation.

Picking channels: Amazon vs. Sephora vs. brick-and-mortar

  • Stingler says brick-and-mortar remains the single largest beauty sales channel.
