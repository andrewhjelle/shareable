01L'Oréal India by the numbers

L'Oréal India by the numbers

  • Lebel gives L'Oréal's global revenue as 44 billion euros last year against an India business of about 600 million euros, with India market share around 7%, roughly half the group's global average.
  • He says the addressable Indian consumer base, defined by disposable income, is projected to reach 450 million by 2030.

02Acquisition playbook

Acquisition playbook

  • Lebel says L'Oréal has acquired 39 brands across multiple decades, most recently three in 2025.
  • He states L'Oréal will not buy a business purely for its revenue stream, and lays out criteria including strategic fit within categories or segments where L'Oréal doesn't already play, and scalability.

03Category and channel bets

Category and channel bets

  • Lebel calls quick commerce largely an Indian invention that he expects to become as large as some of L'Oréal's biggest divisions.
  • On category strategy, he says brands that win in skin tend on average to be more specialist than generalist.

04Lower cost of experimentation

Lower cost of experimentation

  • Lebel contrasts today's environment with an earlier era when testing a new product required a large-scale physical launch, saying the cost of failure has fallen because brands can now test via D2C and single-platform marketing before committing further.
