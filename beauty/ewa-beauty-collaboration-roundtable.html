01Why founders say collaboration beat competing alone

Why founders say collaboration beat competing alone

  • Panelists open by defining collaboration as power, community-building, and a way to solve complex problems no single person can address alone.
  • One founder says partnering with marketers, manufacturers, and formulators who had already 'gone ahead' of her shortened her learning curve, and that beauty-institute training and international certifications were what let her advance and be trusted by peers and clients.

02Trust built a brand before packaging did

Trust built a brand before packaging did

  • A haircare founder describes selling early products, like a satin crib bumper and a pre-wash conditioner, with no label or packaging at all.
  • She attributes the sales entirely to the trust built by years of free step-by-step education content on Instagram.

“the loyalty and trust that I had built with my audience made them buy it”

said at 16:17
03Barriers: no paper trail, fear of sharing

Barriers: no paper trail, fear of sharing

  • Asked about barriers to collaboration in Nigeria's beauty industry, one panelist points to a culture of verbal agreements that break down without documentation, describing having to bring in a lawyer to formalize an influencer partnership.
  • Another names fear of sharing information and know-how once a business starts succeeding as a separate, common barrier.

04Reformulating a stigmatized product: African black soap

Reformulating a stigmatized product: African black soap

05Unqualified advice is why consumers distrust science-backed skin care

Unqualified advice is why consumers distrust science-backed skin care

  • A cosmetic scientist argues that hesitancy toward science-backed skin care in Africa largely traces to bad experiences with unqualified advice, such as untrained voices on Instagram.
  • Her proposed fix is collaboration between researchers who generate clinical data, creators who simplify it, and brands that formulate honestly and specifically for African skin.
