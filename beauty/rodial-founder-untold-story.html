If you only read one thing

  • Hatzistefanis started Rodial in 1999 with 20,000 pounds of her own savings after no investor would fund her, since she had just been fired from banking with no beauty experience (c1, c2).
  • Naming an anti-aging serum 'snake serum' after its viper-venom-derived peptide turned a plain product into a viral, press-covered hit, a naming pattern Rodial repeated with 'dragon's blood' and 'bee venom' (c3, c4).
  • She says influencer ROI has collapsed since COVID: a kit that once sold 2,000 units per influencer post now sells around 50 (c5).

04Two brushes with financial collapse

Two brushes with financial collapse

  • Hatzistefanis describes losing an Australian luxury retail account after 18 months because Rodial, then just herself and a part-time accountant, couldn't produce the multi-year business and launch plans the retailer required.
  • Separately, a 20-product seasonal tanning range built for one retailer left Rodial with three years of unsold stock and in the red, a decision she says she made against her own instinct.
  • She says she has considered quitting many times.
